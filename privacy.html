Data we collect

When you sign in:

  • name and email: via Google, GitHub, Telegram, or email sign in;
  • phone number: only when you choose WhatsApp code sign in.

When you submit a business listing:

  • name in Arabic and English, description, phone, city, category, and subcategory;
  • address and map pin, and directions to the business.

When you publish a product:

  • name, description, price, currency, category, stock, and discount;
  • up to two photos that you upload (stored in our database).
Photo upload for business listings is currently switched off. Photos remain only on marketplace products; you can delete a product with its photos at any time.

Usage data:

  • views of listings and products: one count per visitor per 24 hours;
  • likes and reports on listings;
  • comments, reviews, and reward code use inside the app;
  • random deviceId in your browser: we use it to count repeats and apply rate limits; it holds no personal details;
  • brief error reports: message, page, device, and browser type.

What we do not collect

  • passwords for your Google, GitHub, or Telegram accounts: we never see or store them;
  • payment details: the service is fully free, there are no payments;
  • your activity on other websites.

Why we collect it

  • to show listings and products to every visitor: that is the point of the service;
  • to run your account: sign in, saved places, reward codes;
  • to bring your name and email over from Google, GitHub, or Telegram with your consent;
  • to fight spam with rate limits: 10 submissions per day for guests, 30 for signed-in users, 20 per IP address;
  • to count views and ratings for the ranking;
  • to fix bugs fast using error reports.

Where the data lives and for how long

  • storage: Cloudflare (USA) in a D1 database and CDN cache;
  • site pages: GitHub Pages (Microsoft, USA); every connection uses HTTPS;
  • listings and products stay until you delete them, or an admin removes them;
  • error logs stay only while we need them for debugging;
  • rate limit counters reset automatically each day at 00:00 UTC.

Who sees your data

Business listings are public by design: every directory visitor sees them.

The site admin sees the admin panel: listings, products, reviews, reports, sign-in code logs, and user counters.

We do not sell data and do not pass it to ad networks. Ads on the site lead onward only when you tap them.

WhatsApp handoff: when you tap the contact button, the browser opens a chat on whatsapp.com; WhatsApp privacy rules apply from that point.

Third parties

  • Cloudflare: hosting and database;
  • GitHub Pages: site pages;
  • Google, GitHub, Telegram: sign-in options; each sends us your name and email only after your consent in their window;
  • WhatsApp: reached from our contact button.

Your rights

  • view and edit your data in your account;
  • fix a listing or product in «My listings»;
  • delete a listing, a product, or your whole account in profile settings;
  • request removal by email; we respond within 7 days.

Children

The service is for people aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from younger children. If we learn an account belongs to a child, we delete it.

Security

  • all traffic runs over encrypted HTTPS;
  • passwords for email accounts are stored as hashes and are never readable by anyone, including admins;
  • code sign in limits the number of attempts;
  • only the admin can access the database.
No system is 100% safe. Keep private details out of public listings.
